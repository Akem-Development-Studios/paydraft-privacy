Pay Draft – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Pay Draft ("the app", "we") is an invoicing and receipt app for small businesses. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Data you enter stays on your device

Your business profile (name, address, phone, email, logo, Mobile Money and bank details), clients, invoices, receipts and generated PDFs are stored locally on your device only (in the app's private storage). We do not run servers that receive this data and we do not have access to it.

PDFs leave your device only when you choose to share or save them (for example via WhatsApp, email, or the Downloads folder). The app you share with handles the data under its own privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Android's system backup may back up app data to your Google account if you have backup enabled.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Premium users do not see ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time Premium purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card or Mobile Money details. Google shares a purchase token with the app so it can unlock Premium; this is checked on your device.

See Google Play's terms and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data or your clients' data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties other than the Google services described above.
  • We do not use your invoice data for advertising.

5. Children

Pay Draft is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Tax disclaimer

Pay Draft does not provide tax advice and is not URA EFRIS-certified; you are responsible for tax compliance.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
